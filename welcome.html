FormPilot

Installed. Here is what it does and what it will never do.

Three things, all on your device

1 Fills forms it understands It works out what each box wants from its label, not from a list of websites. It never submits the form, never fills a password, and never overwrites something you already typed.
2 Resizes a photo into a portal's exact range “JPEG, max 600px, between 20 and 50 KB” — including the minimum, which is the half most compressors ignore and the reason uploads get rejected.
3 Reads your ID cards to save typing PAN, Aadhaar and marksheets, recognised on this device. Every suggestion is editable and nothing is saved until you say so.

No account. No server. No network requests of any kind — open DevTools and watch, everything you see should be a chrome-extension:// URL.

Before you choose a passphrase

Your passphrase cannot be recovered. Not by you, not by anyone — there is no reset link, no security question and no support address that can help, because nothing about your vault leaves this device. Forget it and the data is gone. Pick something you will still know in a year, and export a backup once you have set things up.

Not ready to commit anything yet? Try it on a sample form first. It uses made-up details and never touches a vault.
